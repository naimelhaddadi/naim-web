  1. 01Problem

    A new clinic, and a lot of messages.

    A newly opened private dental clinic needed a website, and a way to handle appointment communication with its patients.

  2. 02Manual process

    Appointments, confirmations, reminders.

    Every appointment means the same conversation: confirm it, remind the patient, handle the reply. Predictable, repetitive work.

  3. 03Automation

    n8n at the centre.

    Webhooks bring appointment events into n8n, where the workflows for appointments, confirmations and reminders live.

  4. 04Communication

    Through WhatsApp, with an LLM.

    Messages are sent and received through the WhatsApp Business API, with an LLM integrated into the conversation.

  5. 05Status

    Finished and tested.

    The whole flow is finished and tested on a Linux VPS that I administered. It hasn't gone into production yet.
